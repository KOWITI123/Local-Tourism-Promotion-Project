Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Pori uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on our website. By continuing to use our platform, you consent to the use of cookies as described below. We value your privacy and provide options to manage your preferences.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us recognize you, remember your preferences, and improve site functionality. They can be session cookies (temporary) or persistent cookies (stored for longer periods), depending on their purpose.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for essential functions like maintaining your session during bookings, analyzing traffic to optimize performance, and delivering personalized content. Marketing cookies may track your interests for targeted ads, but you can opt out via our cookie settings.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Our site uses necessary cookies for operation, performance cookies for analytics, and optional advertising cookies. Third-party cookies from partners like Google Analytics may also be present to provide insights into user behavior, always in compliance with privacy laws.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings, deleting them or blocking new ones. On our site, we offer a cookie consent banner where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies. Note that disabling necessary cookies may affect site functionality.

5. Third-Party Cookies

We partner with third parties who may set their own cookies, such as for advertising or social media integration. These are governed by their privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. We do not control these cookies but ensure transparency in their use.

6. Updates to This Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements. Any revisions will be posted here with an updated date, and we recommend checking back regularly. Your continued use signifies acceptance of the latest version.

For questions or to adjust your preferences, contact cookies@pori.com. This policy was last updated on March 16, 2025.
