Visitor Information

Everything you need to know for your trip to Kenya.

Travel Essentials

Visas
  • Kenya has implemented an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) system. Visitors must obtain an eTA before travelling.
  • A valid passport with sufficient blank pages (typically at least six months of validity).
  • Depending on nationality, there may have been visa requirements in the past, but the eTA has changed much of that. Always check the most up-to-date information.
  • May be required to show proof of onward travel.
  • For Kenyan Citizens, proof of citizenship (National ID or Passport).
  • No visa or eTA requirements.
  • Access to citizen/resident rates at national parks and attractions.
Health
  • Yellow fever vaccination certificate may be required if arriving from certain countries.
Currency
  • Currency: Kenyan Shilling (KES)

Safety Tips

Stay safe by keeping valuables secure, avoiding isolated areas at night, and following local guidelines. Always carry a copy of your passport and travel insurance details.
