Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to our comprehensive FAQ section, designed to address the most common inquiries from travelers planning their journey to Kenya with Pori. Below, we’ve compiled detailed answers to help you prepare for an unforgettable adventure, covering topics from travel logistics to cultural experiences and safety concerns.

What are the best times to visit Kenya?

The best times to visit Kenya depend on your interests. The dry seasons (June to October and January to February) are ideal for wildlife safaris, especially in the Maasai Mara, where the Great Migration occurs. These periods offer clear skies and easier game viewing. The rainy seasons (March to May and November to December) bring lush greenery and fewer crowds, making them great for cultural tours and coastal visits, though some roads may be less accessible. Always check weather updates closer to your travel date for the most accurate planning.

Do I need a visa to enter Kenya?

Kenya has implemented an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) system, requiring most visitors to obtain an eTA online before travel. Ensure your passport is valid for at least six months and has sufficient blank pages. Some nationalities may have different requirements, so it’s wise to verify the latest information on the official Kenyan government website. Kenyan citizens need a National ID or passport, and residents enjoy discounted rates at parks.

What should I pack for my trip?

Packing depends on your itinerary. For safaris, bring binoculars, lightweight clothing, a hat, sunscreen, and sturdy shoes. Coastal trips require swimwear, flip-flops, and light layers. Include a raincoat for wet seasons, a first-aid kit, and a power adapter for Kenyan sockets. Don’t forget your camera to capture the stunning landscapes and wildlife encounters!

Is Kenya safe for tourists?

Kenya is generally safe for tourists, especially in popular areas like Nairobi, Mombasa, and national parks. However, exercise caution in crowded places, avoid walking alone at night, and follow local advice. Pori recommends traveling with a guide in remote areas and keeping valuables secure. Check travel advisories before departure for the latest safety updates.
