Privacy Policy

At Pori, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, book a trip, or interact with our services. By using Pori’s platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our services. This includes personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, and passport information when you book a trip. We also gather non-personal data like IP addresses, browser types, and usage patterns to enhance user experience. Cookies and similar technologies may be used to track your preferences and interactions on our site.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process bookings, send confirmations, and provide customer support. We may also use it for marketing purposes, such as sending promotional emails about new tours or special offers, but only with your consent. Analytical data helps us optimize our website and tailor content to your interests, ensuring a seamless travel planning experience.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. However, we may share data with trusted partners, such as travel operators or payment processors, to fulfill your bookings. In cases required by law or to protect our rights, we may disclose information to government authorities. All partners are bound by confidentiality agreements to protect your data.

4. Data Security

We implement robust security measures, including encryption and secure servers, to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Despite these efforts, no online transmission is fully secure, so we encourage you to safeguard your account credentials and report any suspicious activity immediately.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe link in our emails. If you have concerns about how we handle your data, contact us, and we’ll address them promptly in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

6. International Data Transfers

As a Kenya-based company, your data may be transferred to and processed in other countries. We ensure that any international transfer complies with legal standards, such as the EU’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for European users, through appropriate safeguards like standard contractual clauses.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated date, and we encourage you to review it periodically. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

For further questions, contact us at privacy@pori.com. This policy was last updated on March 16, 2025.
