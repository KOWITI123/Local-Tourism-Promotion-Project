Maasai Mara National Reserve

Maasai Mara National Reserve is a renowned wildlife conservation area known for its exceptional populations of lions, leopards, cheetahs, and the annual Great Migration. Covering over 1,500 square kilometers, the reserve offers a peaceful escape into nature's wonders, with opportunities to witness wildlife, experience Maasai culture, and explore diverse landscapes.

When to go

Learn more →

Kenya experiences warm days and cool nights, perfect for year-round adventures. The dry seasons (June-October and January-February) are ideal for wildlife viewing, while the rainy seasons bring lush landscapes and fewer tourists.

Getting here

Learn more →

Kenya is easily accessible with international flights to Nairobi and Mombasa. Major wildlife reserves are connected by domestic flights and well-maintained roads. Nairobi serves as the main hub, making it convenient to explore Kenya's diverse regions.
